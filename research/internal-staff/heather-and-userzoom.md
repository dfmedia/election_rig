Run the Election Rig interviews with our in-house newsroom staff as a joint CRUX and Customer Experience study: Karl and Heather Dzierson co-run the sessions in UserZoom between Nov 16 and Dec 4. ## Why include Heather

1. **She builds our research setups in UserZoom.** Heather sets up studies in the platform and works directly with UserZoom support, so the sessions, consent terms and recordings are ready in days, not weeks, and every session runs the same way.
2. **Two researchers fit the deadline.** One moderates while the other takes notes, so 10 to 14 sessions fit before Dec 4, and two people reviewing findings catches more than one.

## Why UserZoom

1. **It's already paid for.** Our contract runs through December 2026, and the interviews finish Dec 4. No procurement, no new security review.
2. **We've already run moderated sessions in it.** That includes the Denver Post user feedback sessions in December 2025 and a moderated app test the same month.
3. **It's built for research, unlike a regular video call.** Recordings, consent terms and highlight reels live in one place, so the findings readout can include short clips of editors at work.

One thing to check: UserZoom is being folded into UserTesting, where moderated interviews are called Live Conversations. Heather will confirm that our plan includes moderated sessions before we book participants. Newsroom staff are colleagues, so no panel or incentives are needed.

**A note on the contract renewal:** our UserZoom contract runs through December 2026, and we haven't decided yet whether to renew UserZoom or move to Maze. Either way, if the interviews have to wait until that's settled, both platforms have tools for analyzing and visualizing findings. A regular video call recording does not.

## How it would work

- **One participant list.** Karl and Heather recruit with Diana and Kalani, combining the CRUX interviews with Diana's discovery meetings so power users are asked once.
- **Who we talk to:** 10 to 14 newsroom editors (on Election Rig and on other tools, including Denver), central setup staff and print editors.
- **The sessions:** 18 minutes each in UserZoom, using the existing moderator guide. Karl and Heather take turns moderating and taking notes.
- **Heather's time:** about 7 hours of sessions plus her share of the synthesis, an estimate to confirm with her.
- **What you get:** session notes by Dec 4, then a findings readout with clips by Dec 18, in time for the "workflow defined, platform decided" gate.
