# Reader (customer) research — Discover phase

Not started. Planned for early 2027, alongside the technical spikes. The current Align round interviews in-house staff only.

## Planned methods

- **Analytics:** Election Rig dashboard (Nov 3–6, 2026 baseline), page performance by technology (Confluence DAT), 2025 local-race and voter-guide traffic.
- **Survey or unmoderated test** in Maze (or UserZoom, pending the renewal decision).
- **Prototype tests** with readers before and during build.

## Baseline (Election Rig dashboard, Nov 3–6, 2026, via kickoff deck)

- ~290,000 views and ~210,000 visits on ~200 results pages.
- ~29 seconds per visit; 1 in 6 visits went on to another story.
- 224 newsletter signups and 13 subscriptions started from those pages.
- Top pages: Virginian-Pilot Virginia local (~48,000 views), Sun Sentinel South Florida (~30,000), Orlando Sentinel Florida (~23,000).

## Questions to answer (from the case doc)

- Where readers look for results on election night, and what they expect to find there.
- Which results views they could read at a glance, and which they had to puzzle over.
- What made them leave a results page, or come back to it.
- Assumption 5: more charts and maps, or faster, simpler, more legible results?
